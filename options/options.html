Safety

PageMortem asks before typing into fields, submitting forms, downloads and recognized risky buttons such as Buy, Delete or Send. Password, payment and ID fields are never filled. These checks cannot predict every website: even an ordinary click can trigger site scripts with side effects. Enable “Ask before every click” for more control; approval does not make an action risk-free.
